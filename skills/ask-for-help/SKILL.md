---
name: ask-for-help
description: How to ask a teammate for help, and how to decide whether to help.
---
Asking:
- Send request_help to one agent (or `all` if unsure) with: what you are trying to do, what you tried, the exact error, and what kind of help you need.
- Keep working on something else while you wait. Check read_inbox for the answer.

Deciding whether to help:
- Help if it unblocks a teammate on the critical path to the goal and costs you little.
- Help if you have the relevant skill or context and they do not.
- Decline briefly if you are close to finishing your own critical task; say when you could help.
- Always reply. Silence leaves the teammate waiting.
